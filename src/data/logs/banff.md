## Background

Since a young age, I have been fortunate enough to have traveled across many places thanks to my parents. We used to visit Pakistan once a year while squeezing in other countries often as well. Starting from America to UK and exploring majority of Europe up until 2018 when I turned 13. Afterwards, the great Covid took place and we were stuck in Qatar for 1-2 years. In 2022 I graduated and moved to Canada for university which was my first time in Canada.

Now I’d be lying if I said I hadn’t ‘traveled’ or gone on ‘vacation’ since 2018 and 2026. I went to Pakistan in 2018 & 2019, started exploring Ontario in 2022, went to Turkey in 2023, and explored Quebec and northern Ontario 2024 and onwards. In 2024, my family even went to Mt Tremblant for a week where I did my first ever hike - that being climbing the entire mountain. I was dead.

## The trip

A few weeks ago we went to Banff National Park in Alberta. We had 2 cottages in Golden BC to accommodate all 19 of us. With such a large number of people, it gets quite difficult to plan to ensure everyone’s happiness especially considering that we have people of all ages from 7 till 70. Our family isn’t the best with planning either :p

Despite having nearly nothing planned and a few almost extinction-event level family arguments, I’d say this was still one of if not the best trip I’ve ever been on.
